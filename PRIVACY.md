# Uttr Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 23 September 2026_

Uttr is a browser extension made by Ajay Alle that reads selected text aloud. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it. In short: **Uttr doesn't collect, store or share any personal data.**

## What Uttr accesses

- **The text you select on a webpage.** When you select text, Uttr passes it straight to your browser's built-in speech engine (the Web Speech API) so it can be read aloud. The text isn't saved, logged or sent anywhere by Uttr.
- **Your Uttr settings**: on/off, speed and voice. These are saved with your browser's extension storage (`storage.sync`), so they're kept between sessions. If you're signed in to your browser with sync turned on, your browser may sync these three settings across your devices. That's handled by your browser, not by Uttr.

## What Uttr does NOT do

- ❌ It doesn't collect personal information, browsing history or website content.
- ❌ It doesn't use analytics, cookies, trackers or advertising.
- ❌ It doesn't send data to the developer or any third party. Uttr has no servers.
- ❌ It doesn't sell or share data with anyone.

## Voices provided by your browser

Uttr uses the voices your browser and operating system provide. Some voices, such as Chrome's voices named "Google …", are online voices. When you choose one, **your browser** may send the text to its provider to generate the audio, in the same way it would for any website that uses speech. This is governed by your browser's own privacy policy. To keep everything on your device, choose a voice that isn't labelled as an online voice.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| `storage` | To remember your three settings |
| Access to websites (content script) | To detect when you select text on a page, so it can be read aloud |

## Changes to this policy

If Uttr ever changes how it handles data, this page will be updated before the change is released, and the date above will change.
